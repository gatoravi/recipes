## Ingredients

- Eggs – 5, boiled and halved
- Onion – 2, medium, chopped
- Chilli powder – 2 tbsp
- Turmeric powder – 1/2 tsp
- Salt – to taste
- Oil

## Method

1. Heat oil in a pan, add the chopped onions, and fry 2 minutes. Add salt, chilli powder, and turmeric powder.
2. Add 1 glass of water and bring to a boil, letting it thicken slightly. Place the egg halves yolk-side down in the gravy, cover, and cook 3–5 minutes.
