## Ingredients

- Vegetables – frozen sambar vegetable mix
- Tur dal – 1 (metal tumbler) glass
- Onion – 1 small
- Tomato – 1
- Sambar powder – 1 tbsp
- Chilli powder – 2.5 tbsp (adjust to taste)
- Salt – to taste
- Hing (asafoetida) – 1/2 spoon
- Red chilli – 1; turmeric – 1/2 tsp
- Mustard seeds – 1 tsp; cumin – 1 tsp
- Urad dal – 1 tsp; fenugreek seeds – 1/2 tsp
- Tamarind – 1 small seeded lump

## Method

1. Cook 1 cup tur dal. In a pan, heat 2 tbsp oil and add the tempering (hold off on turmeric). Add onion; once soft, add tomato, then the vegetables.
2. Add turmeric, sambar powder, chilli powder, and salt. Add 1 glass water and bring to a boil. Add the tamarind pulp and boil.
3. Cover and let the vegetables cook (or pressure cook, 1–2 minutes after the first whistle). Mash in the cooked dal, add a little more water to adjust consistency, and simmer 2–3 minutes. Check for taste and switch off.
4. Margin note: don’t let it get too sour — go easy on the tamarind.
