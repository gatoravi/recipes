## Ingredients

- Paneer
- Besan (chickpea flour) [unclear]
- Ginger garlic paste, water [unclear]
- Mawa — roughly 1 tbsp [unclear]
- Ghee or butter
- Onion – 1, medium or small
- Potato – boiled (optional)
- A tempering, about 1 tsp each of a couple of spices [which ones is unclear]
- Garam masala powder – 1/2 tsp
- Green chilli – 1
- Spinach (palak), blended
- Chilli powder, salt, garam masala powder
- Malai or fresh cream (optional)

## Method

1. Cut the paneer into cubes (thaw first if using frozen). Several lines here are illegible.
2. Blend the palak and add to the pan along with blanched potatoes. Add the paneer, chilli powder, salt, and garam masala powder.
3. Add water as needed and boil 3–4 minutes. Malai or fresh cream can be stirred in; boil 2 more minutes and remove. The notebook notes this serves about 10.
