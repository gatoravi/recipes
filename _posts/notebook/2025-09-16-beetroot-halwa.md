## Ingredients

- Grated beetroot – 2 cups
- Sugar – 1 cup (adjust to taste)
- Milk – 2 cups
- Cashews – 3 tbsp
- Cardamom, crushed – 1 tsp
- Ghee – 4 tbsp

## Method

1. Heat 2 tbsp ghee in a pan. Fry the beetroot, add the milk, and cook 7 minutes. Meanwhile, fry the cashews separately in 1 tsp ghee.
2. Once it thickens, add the sugar and cardamom and let it boil. Stir in the remaining ghee and remove.
