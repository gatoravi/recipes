## Ingredients

- Beans, cut lengthwise (or cabbage, chopped) – about 3/4 glass
- Onion – 1/4, finely chopped
- Ginger garlic paste – 1 tbsp
- Salt, turmeric powder
- Mustard seeds, urad dal, red chilli, moong dal — for tempering

## Method

1. Temper oil with mustard seeds, urad dal, and red chilli; add moong dal. Add turmeric powder and salt.
2. When the dal is half-cooked, add the beans (or cabbage), cover, and cook until soft. A few corrected/overwritten lines here weren’t fully legible.
