## Ingredients

- Raw plantain – 2 (peeled, cut into pieces)
- Onion – 2, medium (grind 1 into a paste)
- Garlic – 5 cloves
- Ginger garlic paste – 1 tbsp
- Salt
- Chilli powder – 3 tsp
- Turmeric powder – 1 tsp
- Mustard seeds and urad dal, for tempering

## Method

1. Temper oil with mustard seeds and urad dal. Add the onion paste and fry about 3 minutes. Add the ginger garlic paste and fry another minute.
2. Add the plantain pieces, salt, turmeric, and chilli powder, then 1 glass of water. Cover and cook on low flame until the plantain is soft.
