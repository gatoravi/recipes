## Ingredients

- Idli rice – 4 glasses (soak in the morning)
- Urad dal – 1½ glasses (soak in the afternoon with 1 tsp methi/fenugreek seeds)

## Method

1. Grind the urad dal first, with a little water, to a smooth paste; set aside. Grind the rice in 2 batches to a coarse batter.
2. Combine both in a large vessel, add 2 tsp salt, and mix by hand. Cover and let it rise and ferment — near a radiator or other warm spot — for at least 1.5 days. Use the freshest batter for idli, and the rest for dosa.
