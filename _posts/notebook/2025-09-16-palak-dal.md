## Ingredients

- Spinach – frozen or fresh
- Tur dal – 1/2 cup
- Tomato – 1
- Onion – 1, medium
- Green chilli – 1
- Red chilli – 1
- Garlic – 5 pods
- Ghee or oil

## Method

1. Pressure-cook 1 cup dal with 3 cups water for 2 whistles, then simmer on low about 7 minutes (margin note).
2. Boil the tur dal with turmeric powder, tomato, half the onion (chopped), green chilli, and garlic.
3. In a pan, heat 1 tbsp ghee and temper with mustard seeds and urad dal (and red chilli). Mash the spinach, chop the remaining onion small, and add both to the tempering; cook a few minutes, covered.
4. Stir in the dal mixture, adjust salt, cover, and cook until it thickens. Remove.
