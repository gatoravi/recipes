## Ingredients

- Tur dal – 1/2 cup
- Bengal gram (chana dal) – 1/2 cup
- Moong dal (yellow) – 1/2 cup
- Idli rice – 1/2 cup
- Desiccated coconut / coconut powder – 1/2 cup

## Method

1. Soak everything together overnight. In the morning, add about 4 green chillies and grind coarsely in a mixie to a thick batter.
2. Mix in coconut, salt, and asafoetida powder, and make adai like dosa.
