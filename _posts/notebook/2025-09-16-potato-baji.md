## Ingredients

- Potatoes – 4 or 5
- Onion – 2
- Chillies – 3
- Ginger garlic paste – 1 tsp

## Method

1. Boil the potatoes. In a pan, heat oil and temper with mustard seeds and urad dal. Add onions and ginger garlic paste; sauté lightly until golden.
2. Add salt and 1 tsp turmeric powder, then 2–3 glasses of water. Peel and mash the potatoes; once the water is boiling, add them in. Lower the flame, cover, and cook through.
