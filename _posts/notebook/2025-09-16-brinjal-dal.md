## Ingredients

- Brinjal (eggplant) – 5 or 6, small
- Tomato – 1
- Onion – 1
- Chilli – 1
- Tur dal – 3/4 cup
- Sambar powder – 2 tsp

## Method

1. Chop everything and add to the tur dal with 1 spoon turmeric powder, 2 tsp sambar powder, and salt. Add 2 cups water.
2. Pressure cook for 3 whistles, then mash the dal mixture.
3. For the tempering: heat oil, add mustard seeds, sombu (fennel seeds), and asafoetida powder, and stir into the dal.
