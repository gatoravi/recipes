## Plain (coconut) chutney

### Ingredients

- Coconut – 5 tbsp
- Fried gram (daliya / split roasted gram) – 5 tbsp
- Green chillies – 2
- Salt – to taste

### Method

1. Grind to a smooth consistency. Garnish with tempered mustard seeds and urad dal. Coriander can be added and ground in for variation.

## Peanut chutney

### Ingredients

- Roasted peanuts – 1 glass
- Onion – 1
- Red chilli – 2
- Tamarind – a small ball
- Salt – to taste

### Method

1. Grind everything together, adding water gradually to reach the right consistency.
